# Playbook index

Racing principles distilled from outside sources (listed in `../sources.md`), each tied to what `analyze.py` measures. Use it in step 6: find the flag, read the principle, pick one fix. The judgment in the debrief is yours; the playbook is where the fix comes from.

| What the data shows | Read | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Late at the gun, slow at the gun, flat acceleration | `starts.md` | Time on distance; Acceleration |
| Last maneuver close to the gun, lots of prestart turning | `starts.md` | Final approach |
| Started at the wrong end for the side we then sailed | `starts.md` | Line bias vs. favored side |
| Tacks on lifts, long stretches sailing headed | `upwind-strategy.md` | Oscillating shifts |
| Worked one side while the wind trended the other way | `upwind-strategy.md` | Persistent shifts; Reading the pattern |
| Overstood, reached the layline early, extra distance vs. ideal | `upwind-strategy.md` | Laylines |
| Big leverage, far from the rhumb line, separated from the fleet | `tactics.md` | Leverage and consolidation |
| Many tacks with no shift behind them | `tactics.md`, `boat-handling.md` | Lanes; Tacks |
| Tack speed loss high, slow recovery | `boat-handling.md` | Tacks |
| Gybe loss, messy sets and drops | `boat-handling.md` | Gybes; Sets and drops |
| Rounding metres lost, slow exit, reached off after the windward mark | `roundings.md` | Windward mark |
| Pinched or went wide out of the leeward mark, long settle time | `roundings.md` | Leeward mark and gates |
| Downwind VMG low, sailing too high or too low, extra distance on runs | `downwind.md` | Pressure first; Angles; Shifts |
| Speed or heel off the card, big heel spread | `etchells-technique.md` | Balance and heel; Gears |
| Upwind mode questions (high vs. fast) | `etchells-technique.md` | Modes |

Each file keeps points short: the principle, how it shows in our data, a cue for the boat, and the source. Add new material to the matching file (see "Learning from a link" in `SKILL.md`).
