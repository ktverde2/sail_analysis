# Sept ODW 2026 (Sat 26 Sep, 3 races): Mojo and 969

These are the two boats tracked in Njord. Neither logs wind; speed is SOG.

- `data/<boat>/raceN.csv`: Njord `get_data` at 1 Hz, from gun −7 min to finish +1 min, plus `-race.json` (course) and `-raceInfo.json` (Mojo only)
- `report/<boat>/`: per-boat analysis (`executive.md`, `event.md`, `raceN/report.md` plus plots), `debrief.md`, `report.html`
- `report/fleet.md`, `report/fleet-debrief.md`, `report/fleet.html`: the head-to-head, with race replay and side-by-side stretches

Open the `.html` files in a browser. They work offline.
