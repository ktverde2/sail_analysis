## Fall Regatta 2026 · J/24s: Sat 14 Nov, 2 races
- Two tracked boats, WR and Kud, on windward–leeward courses in the north bay, with the wind about 260–275° magnetic and oscillating. Race 1: W–L–W, downwind finish. Race 2: W–L, upwind finish.
- WR finished ahead both times: by 7 s in Race 1 and by 2:43 in Race 2.
- Where Kud's 2:50 went: 12 s at the starts, 14 s *gained* upwind, and 172 s lost on the runs.

**What went well**
- **WR: downwind speed, 0.4 kt faster than Kud at the same angle**
  - Side by side on the runs, WR gained 371 m (51 lengths) in 41 minutes, 435 m (59 lengths) of it on speed.
- **Kud: upwind height, 2:00 gained on both beats of Race 1**
  - Side by side upwind, Kud gained 45 m (6.1 lengths) in 22 minutes: 163 m (22 lengths) gained on course against 118 m (16 lengths) lost on speed.

**Top 3 to work on**
1. **Kud: downwind speed (172 s lost to WR on the runs)**
   - Same heel and angle as WR, 0.4 kt slower. It's the kite, weight or steering.
   - **Next time:** Run beside WR and change one setting at a time until the gap stops growing.
2. **WR: upwind height (2:00 lost to Kud in Race 1)**
   - Faster but lower. 18.7° of heel against Kud's 9.3° on Race 1's second beat.
   - **Next time:** Depower and hike before the heel passes about 12°, then point.
3. **Both: windward roundings (46–124 m (6.3–17 lengths) lost each)**
   - Both boats arrived at 2.1–3.5 kt, slow next to their 4–5 kt on the beats, and took up to 90 s to settle on the run.
   - **Next time:** Foot into the mark, then bear away fully before the set.
