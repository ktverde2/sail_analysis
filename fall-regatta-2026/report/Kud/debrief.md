## Fall Regatta 2026 (Kud): Sat 14 Nov 2026, 2 races
- Windward–leeward courses in the north bay with the wind about 260–270° magnetic. Race 1 was W–L–W with a downwind finish (59 min). Race 2 was W–L with an upwind finish (34 min). There's no wind sensor, so the wind speed is unknown; upwind SOG was 4.4–4.8 kt.
- Of the two tracked J/24s, WR finished ahead in both races: by 7 s in Race 1 and by 2:43 in Race 2. These aren't official results.
- Where the 2:50 went: 12 s at the starts, 14 s *gained* upwind and 172 s lost downwind. The runs decided the day.
- Everything here is judged on getting to the mark: time between marks and VMC (speed toward the mark). Speed and heel only explain it.

**What went well**
- **Race 1 upwind: fastest on both beats, 2:00 gained on WR**
  - Beat 1: 12:28 against WR's 13:25, VMC 2.99 kt against 2.82. We worked the right (80% of the beat right of the rhumb line) and rounded 40 s ahead.
  - Beat 2: 15:25 against 16:28, VMC 3.89 kt against 3.59. We rounded 28 s ahead.
  - Side by side upwind over the day, we gained 45 m (6.1 lengths) on WR in 22 minutes. WR was quicker, but we pointed higher: 163 m (22 lengths) gained on course against 118 m (16 lengths) lost on speed.
- **Race 2 start: the best start of the day**
  - At the pin (3% from the pin end, the favoured end by 27 m (3.7 lengths) to sail), 2 m (0.3 lengths) behind the line at 5.3 kt, crossed 2 s after the gun.
  - First up the first beat at +30 s, +1 min and +2 min, ahead of WR.
- **Leeward roundings: both better than a clean rounding**
  - −16 m (−2.2 lengths) in Race 1 and −21 m (−2.9 lengths) in Race 2: we came out with more VMC than the legs either side.

**Top 3 to work on**
1. **Downwind speed: 172 s lost to WR on the runs, 0.4 kt slower at the same angle**
   - Side by side on the runs, WR gained 371 m (51 lengths) in 41 minutes. 435 m (59 lengths) of that was speed; our angle was slightly *better* (64 m (8.7 lengths) gained on course).
   - Race 1 run 1: 3.13 kt against WR's 3.56 kt, with the same angle to the wind (11.6° and 12.1° off dead downwind): 152 m (21 lengths) lost. Race 2 run 1: 4.31 kt against 4.75: 167 m (23 lengths) lost.
   - Same heel (2–3°) and same angle, so it's the spinnaker, the weight or the steering, not the course.
   - **Next time:** On the first run, sail beside WR or the next boat and change one thing at a time: pole height, guy (pole further aft), sheet eased to the curl, weight forward and to leeward. Keep whatever closes the gap.
   - **Drill:** Two-boat downwind tuning in practice: 3-minute runs side by side, swap one setting per run, and log the gain.
2. **Race 1 start: 22 s late from the pin**
   - 47 m (6.4 lengths) back at −60 s, then stopped: 0.4 kt at −10 s and 1.0 kt at the gun, 11 m (1.4 lengths) back.
   - The pin was the right end (43 m (5.9 lengths) less to sail), but WR crossed 17 s earlier and was 17 m (2.4 lengths) ahead up the beat at +30 s.
   - Race 2 shows the fix: in position earlier, 5.9 kt at −10 s, and across at the gun.
   - **Next time:** Get to your spot by −1:00 with room to leeward, then do a time-on-distance check: at 4 kt a J/24 covers about 2 m a second. Start the build at −10 s, sheeted on.
3. **Windward roundings: 31, 59 and 95 m (4.2, 8.1 and 13 lengths) lost, mostly in the set**
   - It took 59 s (Race 1, second rounding) and 87 s (Race 2) to settle on the run, against 17 s on our best.
   - Both boats came in slow at the top (2.2 kt in Race 2, against 4–5 kt on the beat), so part of this was light air at the mark. WR lost about as much (69, 46, 124 m), so it's not just us.
   - In the 30 s before the mark we gave away 23 m (3.2 lengths) in Race 1 and 32 m (4.3 lengths) in Race 2: pinching up to it.
   - **Next time:** Pole and halyard ready on the layline. Foot to the mark at full speed, and bear away all the way to the run angle before the kite goes up.

*Also worth a look:*
- **Tacks: 31% speed loss and 31 s to recover in Race 1**
  - Two tacks never got back to 95% speed within a minute. In Race 2 it was 24% and 22 s, with less to cost.
- **Race 1 beat 2: 134 s on port headed about 26° from 45:07 without tacking**
  - The wind went 47° right over the beat and we worked the middle. We still gained on WR, but the headed stretch was our biggest single leak upwind.
- **Race 2 beat 1: WR's beat by 65 s**
  - WR sailed 63% of the beat on the lifted tack against our 49%. We sailed 94 s on starboard headed about 11° from 5:42.

**What the wind did, and where we were** (from both boats' GPS tracks, magnetic)
- It oscillated: a swing every 3–4 minutes, 15–23° typical, and up to 30–70° near the windward mark. Race 1 beat 2 trended right 23°. Race 2 beat 1 trended right 40°, but the boats were only 16 m apart across the course, so speed decided it.
- No side paid on its own. The shifts did: the boat on the lifted tack gained.
- **Next time:** A shift of 10° or more that holds for a minute is the new wind. Tack on it, and don't sail headed for more than a minute.
