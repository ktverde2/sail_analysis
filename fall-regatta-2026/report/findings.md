## Fall Regatta 2026 · J/24s: where the boats diverged
- Two tracked boats, WR and Kud, on windward–leeward courses with the wind about 260–275° magnetic and oscillating. Race 1: W–L–W, downwind finish. Race 2: W–L, upwind finish.
- WR finished ahead both times: by 7 s in Race 1 and by 2:43 in Race 2. These are not the official results.
- Kud's 2:50 total gap splits into 12 s at the starts, 14 s *gained* upwind and 172 s lost downwind.

**Top 3 differences**
1. **Downwind: WR 172 s faster on the runs, 0.4 kt quicker at the same angle**
   - Side by side on the runs (41 minutes), WR gained 371 m (51 lengths): 435 m (59 lengths) on speed, 64 m (8.7 lengths) back to Kud on angle.
   - Heel and angle to the wind were alike: 3.1° each and 11.6–12.1° from dead downwind on Race 1's first run.
2. **Upwind: Kud 2:00 faster in Race 1, WR 1:46 faster in Race 2**
   - Side by side upwind (22 minutes), Kud gained 45 m (6.1 lengths): 163 m (22 lengths) on height, 118 m (16 lengths) lost on speed.
   - WR heeled more on every beat (9.6–13.5° against 8.9–10.0°), and 18.7° against 9.3° on Race 1's second beat, port tack.
   - Race 2 beat 1: WR 63% of the beat on the lifted tack, Kud 49%.
3. **Starts: WR across 17 s earlier in Race 1, Kud 5 s earlier in Race 2**
   - Race 1: WR 2 m back at 2.7 kt in the middle of the line; Kud 11 m back at 1.0 kt near the pin, 22 s late.
   - Race 2: Kud 2 m back at 5.3 kt at the pin, first up the beat for the first two minutes; WR 4 m back at 5.7 kt, 7 s late.
