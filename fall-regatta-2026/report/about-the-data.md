## About the data

**Event**
- Fall Regatta 2026, Saturday 14 November 2026, San Diego Bay. Two races, J/24s.
- Race 1: gun 12:54. Windward – leeward – windward, downwind finish.
- Race 2: gun 14:38. Windward – leeward, upwind finish.
- Two boats tracked: Kud and WR. Order, gaps and "fastest" are between these two boats only. They are not the official results.

**Source and sensors**
- Velocitek `.vtk` logs, one per boat: GPS position, speed and course over ground, and heading, heel and trim at about 5 Hz, resampled to 1 s.
- Speeds are over the ground (SOG). There's no paddlewheel, so current is included in every speed.
- Neither boat has a wind sensor. The wind direction is worked out from the boats' tacks and gybes. The wind speed is not known.
- Directions are magnetic (variation 10.7° E).
- Boat lengths are J/24 lengths: 7.32 m. The mark zone is three lengths (22 m).

**Race timer**
- Both boats' timers agree on the two guns.
- WR's timer also ran to zero at 12:30, 12:37, 12:45, 14:20 and 14:32. Those were not J/24 starts and are not analysed.

**Course**
- The logs hold no marks or finish line. Marks and finishes were estimated from both boats' tracks and then placed by hand on the course map.
- Start line: Kud pinged both ends before Race 1 and re-pinged the pin end at 14:11. At the gun in both races, both boats were 23–26 m on the course side of the pinged line. The start lines used here were moved by hand to the actual line positions.
- Both boats pass within 7 m of every mark and cross every finish line.

**Wind and tide**
- No wind data from the boats. The nearest station with data for the race times was the NOAA station on the Scripps pier at La Jolla, about 15 km away on the open coast. It reported 3–5 kt from 280–320° with gusts to 6 kt. That's not necessarily what the race area had.
- Tide at San Diego (NOAA 9410170): high water 10:41 (5.2 ft), low water 19:01 (0.7 ft), so the tide was falling through both races. The current prediction is for North Island (NOAA PCT0056).

**Limits**
- GPS positions are good to a metre or two. Distances to the line and to marks are approximate at that level.
- The data can't show traffic, other boats, crew actions, sail settings or wind pressure.
