## Fall Regatta 2026 (WR): Sat 14 Nov 2026, 2 races
- Windward–leeward courses in the north bay with the wind about 260–275° magnetic. Race 1 was W–L–W with a downwind finish (60 min). Race 2 was W–L with an upwind finish (31 min). There's no wind sensor, so the wind speed is unknown; upwind SOG was 4.4–5.0 kt.
- Of the two tracked J/24s, we finished ahead of Kud in both races: by 7 s in Race 1 and by 2:43 in Race 2. These aren't official results.
- Where the margin came from: 12 s at the starts and 172 s on the runs. Kud gained 14 s on us upwind (2:00 in Race 1, but we took back 1:46 in Race 2).
- Everything here is judged on getting to the mark: time between marks and VMC (speed toward the mark). Speed and heel only explain it.

**What went well**
- **Downwind speed: 172 s gained on the runs, 0.4 kt faster at the same angle**
  - Side by side on the runs, we gained 371 m (51 lengths) on Kud in 41 minutes. 435 m (59 lengths) of it was speed.
  - Race 1 run 1: 3.56 kt against 3.13, 152 m (21 lengths) gained, from 40 s behind at the windward mark to 35 s ahead at the leeward. Race 2 run 1: 4.75 kt against 4.31, 167 m (23 lengths) gained.
  - Whatever we're doing with the kite and weight is right. Write it down.
- **Race 2 first beat: 65 s faster than Kud, from behind at the start**
  - VMC 3.22 kt against 2.92. We sailed 63% of the beat on the lifted tack (Kud 49%) and rounded 60 s ahead.
- **Leeward roundings: −50 m (−6.9 lengths) in Race 1**
  - Better than a clean rounding, and the best leeward rounding of either boat. Back to 90% of upwind VMC in 13 s.

**Top 3 to work on**
1. **Upwind in Race 1: 2:00 lost to Kud over the two beats, faster but lower**
   - Beat 1: 13:25 against 12:28 (VMC 2.82 against 2.99 kt). Beat 2: 16:28 against 15:25 (3.59 against 3.89 kt).
   - Side by side upwind over the day, Kud gained 45 m (6.1 lengths) on us in 22 minutes. We gained 118 m (16 lengths) on speed but lost 163 m (22 lengths) on course: our track was further off the wind.
   - Race 1 beat 2, port tack: we carried 18.7° of heel against Kud's 9.3°, and Kud gained 25 m (3.4 lengths). Our heel on that beat varied ±6.8°, the most of any leg.
   - **Next time:** Sail flatter: hike hard and depower (backstay, traveller down) before the heel passes about 12°. Then point. Judge it on the gap to the boat beside you, not the speedo.
2. **Starts: right spot both times, but slow across**
   - Race 1: 2 m (0.2 lengths) back at the gun, but at only 2.7 kt; crossed 5 s late, in the middle (36% from the pin, 16 m (2.1 lengths) more to sail than the pin).
   - Race 2: 27 m (3.7 lengths) over at −60 s, then 4 m (0.5 lengths) back at 5.7 kt, but crossed 7 s late at 18%. At that speed we should have crossed in about 1 s, so we were running down the line. Kud, at the pin, was 20 m (2.8 lengths) ahead at +30 s.
   - **Next time:** Hold a gap to leeward, and at −8 s turn up to close-hauled and go. Full speed, pointed at the line, at the gun.
3. **Windward roundings: 69, 46 and 124 m (9.5, 6.3 and 17 lengths) lost**
   - Race 2: gave away 60 m (8.2 lengths) in the 30 s before the mark, then took 58 s to settle on the run. Race 1 first rounding: 92 s to settle.
   - Both boats came in slow at the top (2.1–2.2 kt, against 4–5 kt on the beat), so the windward mark looks light. Kud lost about as much (31, 59, 95 m).
   - **Next time:** Foot into the mark at full speed rather than pinching up to it. Pole and halyard ready on the layline, and bear away to the run angle before the set.

*Also worth a look:*
- **Tacks: 33% speed loss and 45 s to recover in Race 1**
  - Three tacks never got back to 95% within a minute. In Race 2 it was 27% and 26 s.
- **Race 1 beat 2: worked the right, and lost 63 s on it**
  - The wind trended right 23°. We were 86 m (12 lengths) further right than Kud and still lost. The shift alone was worth about 18 s, so the rest was speed and height. We also sailed 91 s on port headed about 18° from 36:58.

**What the wind did, and where we were** (from both boats' GPS tracks, magnetic)
- It oscillated: a swing every 3–4 minutes, 15–23° typical, and up to 30–70° near the windward mark. Race 1 beat 2 trended right 23°. Race 2 beat 1 trended right 40°, but the boats were only 16 m apart across the course, so speed decided it.
- No side paid on its own. The shifts did: in Race 2 we spent 63% of beat 1 on the lifted tack and won the beat by 65 s.
- **Next time:** Keep doing what Race 2 did: tack on headers of 10° or more, and don't sail headed for more than a minute.
