## WR: where the boats diverged
- Two races against one other tracked J/24, Kud. WR finished ahead in both: by 7 s in Race 1 and 2:43 in Race 2. These are not the official results.
- The 2:50 total margin splits into 12 s at the starts, 14 s *lost* upwind and 172 s gained downwind.
- Everything below is measured against Kud, on time between marks and VMC (speed toward the mark).

**Top 3 differences**
1. **Downwind: 172 s ahead of Kud on the runs, 0.4 kt faster at the same angle**
   - Run times: Race 1 run 1, 19:20 against 20:35 (−75 s); Race 1 run 2, 10:23 against 10:58 (−35 s); Race 2 run, 13:55 against 14:57 (−62 s).
   - Side by side on the runs (41 minutes): WR gained 371 m (51 lengths). Speed accounts for 435 m (59 lengths); Kud gained 64 m (8.7 lengths) back on angle.
   - Race 1 run 1, side by side: 3.56 kt against 3.13 kt, at 12.1° and 11.6° from dead downwind, both at 3.1° of heel. WR gained 152 m (21 lengths).
   - Race 2 run, side by side: 4.75 kt against 4.31 kt, at 1.5° and 6.0° from dead downwind. WR gained 167 m (23 lengths).
2. **Upwind: behind Kud by 2:00 in Race 1, ahead by 1:46 in Race 2**
   - Race 1: beat 1 in 13:25 against 12:28 (VMC 2.82 against 2.99 kt); beat 2 in 16:28 against 15:25 (3.59 against 3.89 kt).
   - Race 2: beat 1 in 11:33 against 12:38 (VMC 3.22 against 2.92 kt); the beat to the finish in 5:25 against 6:06 (4.24 against 3.80 kt).
   - Side by side upwind (22 minutes): Kud gained 45 m (6.1 lengths). WR gained 118 m (16 lengths) on speed and lost 163 m (22 lengths) on course (height).
   - Average heel by beat: WR 9.9°, 13.5°, 9.6°, 11.5°; Kud 9.2°, 10.0°, 8.9°, 9.7°. On Race 1 beat 2, port tack: WR 18.7°, Kud 9.3°. WR's heel on that beat varied ±6.8°, the most of any leg.
   - Race 1 beat 2: WR was 86 m (12 lengths) further right than Kud as the wind went 23° right; WR took 63 s longer. The shift alone was worth about 18 s.
   - Race 2 beat 1: WR spent 63% of the beat on the lifted tack, Kud 49%.
3. **Starts: 17 s before Kud in Race 1, 5 s after Kud in Race 2**
   - Race 1: 2 m (0.2 lengths) back at 2.7 kt at the gun; crossed 5 s after the gun, 36% from the pin (16 m (2.1 lengths) more to sail than the pin). Kud: 11 m back at 1.0 kt, crossed 22 s after the gun, 13% from the pin.
   - Race 2: 27 m (3.7 lengths) over at −60 s, 4 m (0.5 lengths) back at 5.7 kt at the gun; crossed 7 s after the gun, 18% from the pin. Kud: 2 m back at 5.3 kt, crossed 2 s after the gun, 3% from the pin.
   - Up the first beat: Race 1, ahead of Kud from +30 s to +2 min. Race 2, 14–23 m (1.9–3.1 lengths) behind Kud from +30 s to +2 min.
