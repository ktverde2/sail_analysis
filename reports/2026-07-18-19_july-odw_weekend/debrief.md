## July ODW, July 18–19, 2026 (San Diego)
Five windward-leewards in an 8–10 kt westerly (263–268° Saturday, 275–283° Sunday). Fast boat, good laylines, mostly clean starts. The biggest gain left is in tactics: we tacked on lifts and went right by habit.

**What went well**
- **Speed on target all weekend.** Upwind averaged 100–103% of the Etchells card at 9 kt in four of five races (96% in the light, short race 3 Saturday). Port and starboard were within 0.15 kt of each other on 7 of 9 beats, so current isn't flattering one tack.
- **Laylines.** Six of nine final tacks fetched the mark cleanly. When we were headed, we rarely sat on it: only one stretch all weekend of 45 s or more sailing 5° headed (48 s, Saturday race 2).
- **Sunday race 1 start.** On the line at the gun, mid-line, and accelerating through it (5.0 kt at the gun, 5.8 kt at +10 s). That's the template.

**Top 3 to work on**
1. **Play the shifts, not the side.** Across 29 tacks away from the start and marks, 11 were on a lift and only 5 on a header (13 had no clear shift). Eight of the 11 left a lifted starboard tack to go onto port. We also worked the right side on 8 of 9 beats, 70–100% of the time right of the rhumb line, including all three beats where the wind trended 5–9° left: Saturday race 2's second beat, Sunday race 1's first beat and Sunday race 2's second beat. With the wind oscillating ±3–5°, that's the most expensive habit we have. **Next time:** the trimmer calls the compass every 30 s against the beat's average. Tack when we're 5° headed. Before each beat, decide whether the wind is oscillating or trending, and pick the side from that, not from habit.
2. **Starts: a few seconds late, then one big one.** We crossed 4, 6, 1, 0 and 13 s after the gun. Three of five were within about a boat length. Saturday race 2's 6 s was about two lengths, and the average is still about 5 s. Sunday race 2 cost the most: 77 m back at −60 s, down to 2.1 kt by −30 s with 42 m to go, and still 21 m back at the gun at the boat end. **Next time:** from −30 s, call distance and time out loud and go off the starting-ratio card. 40 m at 5 kt is 16 s, so the go point is about −16 s, not when it feels right.
3. **Tacks lose too much.** Average speed loss per tack was 31–37% on Saturday and 26–29% on Sunday, bottoming around 4.0 kt. Saturday race 3 was the worst: 21 s to recover and about 13 m lost per tack. Sunday's last beat was the deepest (32%). **Next time:** slower helm through the turn, hold the jib until the bow is through, then trim hard. End practice with sets of 10 tacks while tired.

*Also worth a look:* Sunday's heel was 4–6° over the card (22° and 20° against 16° at 9 kt), while Saturday's was within about 2–3°. Speed was fine both days, so either Sunday was windier than 8–10 kt or we're carrying more heel than we need. Try 2 minutes at 16° and 2 at 22° on the first beat of the next practice and compare speeds.

**Race notes**
- **Sat 1:** The best-played race: the two tacks on a real shift were both on headers, with no lift tacks. Four seconds late off the line, but accelerating hard (+1.6 kt through the gun).
- **Sat 2:** Three tacks on lifts. The second beat went 9° left while we were 94% on the right. We also overstood the first windward mark by ~10°.
- **Sat 3:** Near-perfect start timing (1 s), but slow through the gun (−0.7 kt) and the slowest race upwind (96% at 9 kt). Three of the four tacks were on lifts of 5–8° (the fourth was the layline), and they were the deepest of the weekend (37% speed loss).
- **Sun 1:** A great start, then the first beat's wind backed 7° while we worked the right. We overstood the second windward mark by ~11°.
- **Sun 2:** The late start is where most of the time went. Fourteen tacks against seven in race 1; six of them had no clear shift behind them.
