## July ODW, July 18–19, 2026 (San Diego)
Five windward-leewards in an 8–10 kt westerly (263–268° Saturday, 275–283° Sunday). Fast boat, good laylines, mostly clean starts. The distance goes at the marks: 563 m lost in 12 roundings, more than twice what all our tacks and gybes cost. After that, tactics: we tacked on lifts and went right by habit.

**What went well**
- **Speed on target all weekend.** Upwind averaged 100–103% of the Etchells card at 9 kt in four of five races (96% in the light, short race 3 Saturday). Port and starboard were within 0.15 kt of each other on 7 of 9 beats, so current isn't flattering one tack.
- **Laylines.** Six of nine final tacks fetched the mark cleanly. When we were headed, we rarely sat on it: only one stretch all weekend of 45 s or more sailing 5° headed (48 s, Saturday race 2).
- **Sunday race 1 start.** On the line at the gun, mid-line, and accelerating through it (5.0 kt at the gun, 5.8 kt at +10 s). That's the template.

**Top 3 to work on**
1. **Roundings: get out of the mark at speed.** Windward roundings cost 44 m on average and leeward roundings 53 m, about 47 m each (roughly 20 s of upwind sailing). Most of it goes on the way out: 9 of 12 lost more in the minute after the mark than in the 30 s before. At the windward mark, the two worst exits reached off at 130–150° to the wind for 15–40 s instead of bearing away to the run angle (159–170°, where the rest were): Saturday race 1's first mark (53 m) and Saturday race 3's (87 m). The first windward mark of a race always cost more than the second (53 vs 31, 44 vs 24, 51 vs 32 m). At the leeward mark we came out at 4.3–4.8 kt, against 5.7–6.0 kt upwind. Sunday race 1 pinched out (26° to the wind by heading, down to 4.0 kt). Sunday race 2 went wide and slow (43°, down to 3.4 kt) and took 80 s to settle, the worst rounding of the weekend at 84 m. **Next time:** windward, bear away all the way to the run angle first, then set. Leeward, wide in and tight out, and foot for speed until the number reads 5.5 kt before pointing. Drill: six leeward roundings in a row, the trimmer calling speed out of each one.
2. **Play the shifts, not the side.** Across 29 tacks away from the start and marks, 11 were on a lift and only 5 on a header (13 had no clear shift). Eight of the 11 left a lifted starboard tack to go onto port. We worked the right side on 8 of 9 beats, 70–100% of the time right of the rhumb line, including all three beats where the wind trended 5–9° left. **Next time:** the trimmer calls the compass every 30 s against the beat's average; tack when we're 5° headed. Before each beat, decide whether the wind is oscillating or trending and pick the side from that, not from habit.
3. **Starts: a few seconds late, then one big one.** We crossed 4, 6, 1, 0 and 13 s after the gun (about 5 s on average). Sunday race 2 cost the most: 77 m back at −60 s, down to 2.1 kt by −30 s with 42 m to go, and still 21 m back at the gun. **Next time:** from −30 s, call distance and time out loud and go off the starting-ratio card. 40 m at 5 kt is 16 s, so the go point is about −16 s.

*Also worth a look:*
- **Tacks** lose 26–37% of speed and 256 m across the weekend (Saturday race 3 worst, 13 m per tack). Slower helm, jib held until the bow is through.
- **Heel** was 4–6° over the card on Sunday (22° and 20° against 16° at 9 kt) but close on Saturday, with speed fine both days. Test 16° vs 22° for 2 minutes each on the first beat of the next practice.

**Race notes**
- **Sat 1:** The best-played race: the two tacks on a real shift were both on headers, with no lift tacks, and the cleanest leeward rounding of the weekend (22 m). The first windward mark gave back 53 m.
- **Sat 2:** Three tacks on lifts. The second beat went 9° left while we were 94% on the right. We also overstood the first windward mark by ~10°.
- **Sat 3:** Near-perfect start timing (1 s), but slow through the gun (−0.7 kt) and the slowest race upwind (96% at 9 kt). Three of the four tacks were on lifts of 5–8°, the deepest of the weekend (37% speed loss), and the windward mark cost 87 m, the most of any windward rounding.
- **Sun 1:** A great start, then the first beat's wind backed 7° while we worked the right. We overstood the second windward mark by ~11°.
- **Sun 2:** The late start (13 s) and the leeward gate (84 m) are where the time went. Fourteen tacks against seven in race 1; six of them had no clear shift behind them.
