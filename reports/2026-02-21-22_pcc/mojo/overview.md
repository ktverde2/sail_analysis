## Mojo at PCC 2026: the short version
- **33rd of 42 (31-29-38-30-29), 10th of 13 Corinthian.** Saturday 33rd on the day, Sunday 29th.
- **Sunday was a different boat.** Once racing, Mojo matched 1044 upwind and was the fastest of the three tracked boats on 3 of 4 runs, and on race 5's last two beats.
- **The starts cost the most.** Over the line at both Sunday guns. Each restart cost about 45 s, roughly 4–5 places.

**Keep doing**
- Sunday's upwind setup: more heel (19° against 17° on Saturday) and a tighter angle over the ground.
- Downwind speed: in the same breeze as the others, Mojo was the quickest boat on the runs.
- Quick, clean tacks, the best of the three all regatta.

**Work on next**
1. **Starts:** line sights and a bow call in the last 30 s. If in doubt at the gun, dip back at once.
2. **Side choice:** stay between the boats around you and the mark unless a side is clearly paying.
3. **Height upwind:** side by side, 1044 gained on Mojo by pointing higher, not by going faster. Hold height once you're up to speed.
