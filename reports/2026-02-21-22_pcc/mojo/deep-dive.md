## Mojo: race notes
Times are from each race's gun, so you can find them on the fleet report's replays. "Side by side" means within 200 m of another tracked boat on the same leg and the same tack or gybe, in the same breeze (the Side by side page of the fleet report has every stretch).

**Patterns across the regatta**
- **Upwind, side by side: Mojo is fast but low against 1044, and high against Chomp.**
  - Against 1044 (25 min, 9 stretches): 1044 gained 83 m. Mojo was quicker (+81 m from speed) but 1044 made 164 m from height.
  - It's the same stretch after stretch. Race 2 beat 2: Mojo 5.80 kt at 35° to the course, 1044 5.65 kt at 30°. Race 4 beat 2: 5.79 kt at 52° against 5.48 kt at 46°.
  - Against Chomp (13 min): Mojo gained 144 m, almost all from height. In the stretches where it gained most, Chomp was 7–20° lower. 92 m of it came in the last third of the beats, approaching the windward mark.
  - In the Saturday stretches where 1044 gained, it heeled 2–4° more than Mojo (18–23° against 15–19°).
- **Downwind, side by side: the fastest boat, but the highest angle.**
  - Against 1044 (16 min): Mojo gained 32 m, +96 m from speed and −64 m from depth.
  - Against Chomp (39 min): Chomp gained 33 m. Mojo was faster (+29 m) but Chomp sailed deeper (+62 m).
  - Mojo is getting speed by heating up. When it matched the others' depth (race 3's first run, the port gybe of race 5's second run), it gained outright.
- **Tacks: the cheapest of the three.** 28–35% speed loss and 10–16 s back to speed. The costliest race was race 5 (103 m over 14 tacks), the one with the most tacks.
- **Windward roundings: overstanding.** Overstood the layline on 7 of 10, by 6–17°, and lost 42–130 m per rounding including the offset reach. Best: race 3's second (42 m, overstood 17° but tacked only 45 s out).
- **Leeward roundings: the exits vary a lot.** From 50 m lost to 150 m. The best (race 5, first gate) came in at 6.7 kt after gybing 154 s out; the worst (race 3, first gate) came in reaching and wide.

**Race 1 (31st; 3rd of the three)**
- **Start:** middle of the line (42% from the pin). 86 m back at −60 s, 7.5 m at the gun, crossed 5 s late at 5.6 kt. A good start: the late seconds were time-on-distance, not a stall.
- **Beat 1 (14:07):**
  - The wind went right 8° over the beat and Mojo worked the middle (55% right).
  - Starboard was the fast tack (6.11 kt against 5.55 on port).
  - Side by side at 0:25–1:34 with 1044: Mojo was 0.6 kt faster but 9° lower, and 1044 gained 10 m.
- **Windward mark 1:** overstood 11° after a layline tack 58 s out. 104 m lost.
- **Run 1:** no gybes, 79% on port. Side by side with Chomp from 18:07 for 5:37: Chomp gained 64 m (6.12 against 5.88 kt, and deeper).
- **Leeward gate:** right-hand mark, out at 4.6 kt, 43 s to settle, 59 m lost.
- **Beat 2 (15:53):**
  - The slowest beat of the regatta: 5.13 kt at 14.8° of heel (18.8° on beat 1).
  - Worked the left (21% right) in a ±6° oscillation.
  - Side by side with 1044 from 25:02 for 6 min: 1044 gained 38 m, from speed and height both, heeling 2.5° more.
- **Windward mark 2:** came in at 3.8 kt and lost 125 m.
- **Run 2:** 4.89 kt. Side by side with Chomp for 11 min; Chomp gained 26 m on speed.

**Race 2 (29th)**
- **Start:** middle. 2.5 m over at the gun; back at +4 s, restarted at +10 s. A small miss, and it cost little.
- **Beat 1 (16:11):**
  - 88% right, up to 700 m out, with no tacks while 1044 and Chomp went left.
  - Oscillating ±6°. From 13:17, Mojo sailed 66 s on port headed about 10° without tacking.
  - 56 s behind at the first mark.
- **Run 1:** side by side with 1044 twice (16:33 and 19:15). 1044 gained 23 m in total.
- **Leeward gate:** out at 4.3 kt, 48 s to settle.
- **Beat 2 (14:40):**
  - 91% right, steady breeze.
  - Side by side with 1044 for 9 minutes over two stretches (28:06 and 33:39). Mojo was faster both times (5.80 against 5.65 kt, 6.14 against 5.95 kt), but 1044 gained 49 m on height. It carried 21–23° of heel against Mojo's 18.5–19°.
- **Windward mark 2:** layline tack 149 s out, overstood 10°. 113 m lost.

**Race 3 (38th)**
- **Start:** middle, 2 s late at 5.6 kt, the best start of the three boats.
  - It was early: 15 m back at −30 s at only 3.1 kt, then it built to 5.7 kt by −10 s. That worked, but it was tight.
- **Beat 1 (16:17):**
  - 84% right in a ±3° oscillation. 2nd of the three at the first mark.
  - Side by side with 1044 at 2:01: Mojo gained 9 m.
- **Run 1:** side by side with 1044 from 18:26 for 6:40, and Mojo gained 47 m on speed (6.23 against 6.00 kt, same depth). Mojo's best downwind stretch of the regatta.
- **Leeward gate 1: the costliest rounding of the regatta.** Came in reaching at 129° to the wind, 16 m wide of the mark. Left at 67° to the wind doing 2.6 kt and took 91 s to settle: 150 m lost.
- **Beat 2 (12:34):**
  - The wind went left 10° and Mojo was 80% right, on the wrong side.
  - Heel 14.4° and 4.98 kt: flat and slow again.
  - Side by side with Chomp at 27:23: Mojo gained 21 m on height (32° against 44°), though Chomp was faster.
- **Run 2:** three gybes. Chomp gained 22 m on the starboard gybe, then Mojo took 36 m back on port (5.80 against 5.46 kt).
- **Beat 3 (13:08):**
  - The wind went right 25° and Mojo stayed in the middle.
  - Heel 13.7°, 4.61 kt. From 49:51 it sailed 49 s on port headed about 10° without tacking.

**Race 4 (30th)**
- **Start:** pin end (17%), 92 m back at −60 s, 11.8 m at −10 s.
  - 8 m over at the gun at 4.1 kt. It sailed on for about 15 s, was back behind the line at +28 s and restarted at +44 s.
  - The approach was fine at −10 s. The last 10 s at 4.9 kt covered 20 m, so the call to hold back needed to come at about −15 s.
- **Beat 1 (19:15):**
  - The wind went right 9°; Mojo went right (94%). The right call.
  - Heel 18.6°.
  - Side by side with Chomp twice (11:42 and 17:22) and gained 15 m.
- **Windward mark 1:** layline tack 131 s out, overstood 6°. 90 m lost.
- **Run 1:** 6.70 kt, the fastest run of the three.
- **Leeward gate:** out at 40° to the wind, low and wide, at 3.9 kt; 57 s to settle, 84 m lost.
- **Beat 2 (15:56):**
  - Worked the left (31% right) in a ±4° oscillation.
  - Side by side with 1044 at 34:40: Mojo faster (5.79 against 5.48 kt) but 1044 gained 17 m on height.
- **Windward mark 2:** overstood 9°. 130 m lost.
- **Run 2: a 7:51 drag race with Chomp.**
  - From 45:55, 19–49 m apart the whole way down: Mojo gained 11 m (a little faster, a little deeper).
  - At the finish Chomp was 2 s ahead by GPS and Mojo one place ahead officially.

**Race 5 (29th; 2nd of the three)**
- **Start:** 31% from the pin, 1.3 m over at 5.9 kt.
  - It sailed on to 27 m over, was back behind the line at +35 s and restarted at +52 s, 1:29 behind at the first mark.
  - 1.3 m is less than a second at that speed: the smallest possible miss, and the most expensive. 1044 did the same (0.8 m over, restarted at +56 s).
- **Beat 1 (20:16 from the gun):** 91% right, up to 780 m out, in a ±4° oscillation. The long way round after the restart: 165 m more than the ideal distance.
- **Windward mark 1:** overstood 17°.
- **Run 1:** 7.19 kt with two gybes. Side by side with 1044 at 21:56: Mojo gained 14 m at 7.8 kt.
- **Leeward gate 1:** gybed 154 s out and came in at 6.7 kt. Out at 4.9 kt: 50 m lost, Mojo's best rounding of the regatta.
- **Beat 2 (15:54):**
  - The wind went right 16°; Mojo was 82% right. The right call, and the fastest beat of the three.
  - Heel 16.5° on starboard, 23.6° on port. A big difference between tacks: check the traveller and jib car are matched side to side.
- **Run 2:**
  - Chomp gained 25 m on the starboard gybe (deeper).
  - Mojo took 29 m back on port at 6.84 against 6.21 kt, then 17 m more from 52:11.
- **Leeward gate 2:** 167 s to get back to upwind speed, the longest of the regatta, though the metres lost (87 m) were ordinary.
- **Beat 3 (15:14):**
  - Worked the left (4% right) in a steady breeze, and passed Chomp.
  - Side by side with Chomp at 62:45: Mojo gained 20 m on height.
  - Side by side with 1044 at 68:11: Mojo gained 15 m, on height and speed both.
  - Finished 16 s behind 1044.

**Questions for the crew**

- On the runs Mojo was the fastest boat but the highest. Is someone calling pressure so the helm can soak in the puffs?
