## 1044 "Flash": race notes
Times are from each race's gun, so you can find them on the fleet report's replays. "Side by side" means within 200 m of another tracked boat on the same leg and the same tack or gybe, in the same breeze (the Side by side page of the fleet report has every stretch).

**Patterns across the regatta**
- **Upwind, side by side: height over speed.**
  - Against Mojo (25 min, 9 stretches): 1044 gained 83 m, with 164 m from height and 81 m lost on speed.
  - Against Chomp (32 min, 9 stretches): 1044 gained 131 m, with 164 m from height and 33 m lost on speed.
  - Typical: race 2 beat 2 on port, 1044 at 5.65 kt and 30° to the course, Mojo at 5.80 kt and 35°, with 1044 at 21° of heel against 18.5°.
  - The one stretch 1044 lost clearly: race 4 at 10:56 (Chomp pointed 6° higher, +21 m).
  - **Where:** the gains came in open water. In the first two-thirds of the beats 1044 gained 134 m on Chomp and 105 m on Mojo; in the last third, approaching the windward mark, nothing (−3 m and −22 m). With the late layline calls below, the approach is where the height edge goes missing.
- **Downwind, side by side: small losses to both.**
  - Against Mojo (16 min): Mojo gained 32 m. 1044 sailed deeper (+64 m), but Mojo was faster (+96 m).
  - Against Chomp (29 min): Chomp gained 25 m. 1044 was faster (+19 m), but Chomp sailed deeper (+44 m).
  - It's small (1–2 m a minute) and the two boats beat 1044 in different ways. The fix is pressure and soaking, not sail trim.
- **The shortest path upwind:** 26–51% more than the straight line, 34% on average against 41–42% for the others. Few tacks in races 1–2 (3 and 5), then 10–14 a race.
- **Leeward roundings: the weak spot.** Out at 2.3–3.5 kt in 5 of 7, and 41–108 s to settle. The good ones were race 4 (4.9 kt, 41 s) and race 5's second gate (4.9 kt, 53 s).
- **Windward roundings:** late layline calls (2–26 s out on six of ten) and overstanding up to 23°. 56–112 m lost, level with the others.

**Race 1 (12th; 1st of the three)**
- **Start:** pin end (18%), the closest of the three at −60 s (66 m). Slow build: 3.3 kt at −30 s, 4.6 kt at the gun, 4 s late.
- **Beat 1 (13:11):**
  - Left (34% right) in a steady breeze, heel 20.2°, 2 tacks, and only 26% more than the straight line.
  - Side by side with Chomp for 9½ minutes: +95 m, all height (Chomp faster by 0.1–0.4 kt).
  - Side by side with Mojo at 0:25: +10 m.
- **Windward mark 1:** layline tack 101 s out. 66 m lost.
- **Run 1 and leeward gate:** no gybes. Out of the left-hand mark at 3.4 kt and 96 s to settle, but only 56 m lost.
- **Beat 2 (14:18):**
  - Left (19% right) in a ±7° oscillation, one tack. Heel 16.0°, lower than beat 1.
  - From 24:34 it sailed 101 s on starboard headed about 9° without tacking.
  - Side by side with Mojo from 25:02 for 6 min: +38 m.

**Race 2 (16th)**
- **Start:** middle (72%), the furthest back of the three at −60 s (115 m). 17 m back at the gun, 10 s late at 5.6 kt.
- **Beat 1 (15:08):**
  - Left (16% right), one tack. Heel 20.5°.
  - Side by side with Chomp for 7½ min: +16 m. With Mojo at 13:38: −5 m.
  - 4 s behind Chomp at the first mark.
- **Windward mark 1:** layline tack 23 s out, overstood 23°, in at 4.8 kt. 112 m lost, 1044's costliest windward rounding.
- **Run 1:**
  - Chomp gained 42 m side by side from 18:14 (faster and deeper); 1044 made 23 m on Mojo.
  - 29 s slower than Chomp on the leg; no gybes.
- **Leeward gate:** out at 3.4 kt, 73 s to settle, 107 m lost.
- **Beat 2 (14:09): the race winner.**
  - Right (86%), heel 21.2°, 4 tacks. 50 s faster than Chomp, which went left.
  - Side by side with Mojo for 9 minutes (28:06 and 33:39): +49 m.
- **Run 2:** side by side with Chomp from 42:42 for 8 min: +42 m, from speed and depth.

**Race 3 (17th)**
- **Start:** middle (72%). 1 m from the line at −10 s but at only 1.9 kt, then 2.5 m over at the gun. Back at +4 s, restarted at +10 s.
- **Beat 1 (15:43):**
  - Left of the middle (34% right) while Chomp went to the right corner.
  - 7 tacks, heel 18.5°. 44 s ahead of Mojo at the first mark.
- **Windward mark 1:** overstood 22°, but only 56 m lost.
- **Run 1:** no gybes. Mojo gained 47 m side by side from 18:26 (6.23 against 6.00 kt).
- **Leeward gate 1:** out at 3.5 kt, 89 s to settle, 99 m lost.
- **Beat 2 (10:46):** left (13% right) as the wind went left 10°. The right call.
- **Leeward gate 2:** out at 3.1 kt and 108 s to settle, 1044's slowest recovery. 94 m lost.
- **Beat 3 (12:23):** heel down to 16.1°. The tacking angle opened to 93° as the breeze eased: a gear change, and 6–11% faster than Mojo on that beat.

**Race 4 (18th; 1st of the three)**
- **Start:** middle (74%), 1.4 m back at the gun at 5.5 kt, 1 s late. 1044's best start.
- **Beat 1 (18:00):**
  - Left (29% right), heel 20.0°, 4 tacks. 46 s ahead of Chomp at the first mark.
  - Side by side with Chomp: level at 0:26; Chomp gained 21 m at 10:56, pointing 6° higher.
- **Windward mark 1:** layline tack 101 s out. 94 m lost.
- **Run 1:** no gybes. Chomp gained 26 m side by side at 22:11 (7.1 against 6.4 kt).
- **Leeward gate:** out at 4.9 kt, 41 s to settle: 1044's quickest exit and recovery.
- **Beat 2 (15:44):**
  - 62% right, 6 tacks, heel 17.9°.
  - Side by side with Mojo at 34:40: +17 m on height (46° to the course against 52°), with Mojo faster.
- **Windward mark 2:** layline tack 2 s out. 89 m lost.

**Race 5 (26th; 1st of the three)**
- **Start: over, and back.**
  - Middle of the line, 0.8 m over at the gun at 4.9 kt (by the GPS antenna, so the bow was several metres over).
  - It sailed on to about 27 m over, turned back at about +20 s, was behind the line at +36 s and restarted at +56 s, 39% from the pin.
  - Mojo also restarted (at +52 s); only Chomp started cleanly.
- **Beat 1 (19:32 from the gun, 18:36 from the restart):**
  - The wind went right 10° from the restart; 1044 was 66% right. The right call.
  - 48 s behind Chomp at the first mark, having started 53 s after it: from the restart, 1044 was 5 s quicker up the beat.
- **Windward mark 1:** layline tack 6 s out, overstood 19°, 64 s to settle.
- **Run 1:** one gybe, 7.05 kt. +25 m on Chomp side by side at 19:53. Mojo gained 14 m at 21:56, and Chomp 18 m at 26:50.
- **Leeward gate 1:** came in at 5.6 kt and left at 2.3 kt, the slowest exit of the regatta. 99 m lost.
- **Beat 2 (16:06):**
  - Right (86%) as the wind went right 16°. The right call.
  - From 32:50 it sailed 93 s on starboard headed about 12°.
- **Windward mark 2:** overstood 23°, layline tack 16 s out. 101 m lost.
- **Run 2:** gained 17 m on Mojo at 46:02, then Mojo took 11 m back at 52:11. Took the lead of the three back from Chomp.
- **Leeward gate 2:** out at 4.9 kt, 53 s to settle.
- **Beat 3 (15:26):**
  - Left (25% right), 3 tacks, heel 20.0°.
  - Side by side with Chomp at 58:58: +37 m on height. Mojo gained 15 m at 68:11.
  - Finished 16 s ahead of Mojo.
