## Chomp!! at PCC 2026: the short version
- **30th of 42 (29-22-29-31-31), 9th of 13 Corinthian.** Saturday 29th on the day, Sunday 31st.
- **A fast boat that sails low upwind.** Side by side with 1044 and Mojo, Chomp was usually as quick or quicker, but they pointed higher and gained. Most of the 7:11 Chomp gave away to 1044 over the regatta was upwind, and this angle is the main reason.
- **Race 5 showed what a clean start is worth.** 1044 and Mojo both restarted, and Chomp, with more heel (about 19–20°), led the three tracked boats for three marks.

**Keep doing**
- Downwind depth: side by side on the runs, Chomp sailed deeper than both other boats and gained on them.
- Starts: race 2 was on the line with speed, and both Sunday starts were within 6 s.

**Work on next**
1. **Height upwind:** heel to 18–20° and steer for height once the speed is up. Side by side, Mojo gained 144 m on Chomp upwind, almost all on angle.
2. **Fewer, better tacks:** the most tacks of the three boats (8–17 a race) and the slowest recovery (16–27 s). Tack on clear headers only.
3. **Protect a lead:** when ahead, as in race 5, stay between the others and the mark instead of splitting away.
