## Chomp!!: race notes
Times are from each race's gun, so you can find them on the fleet report's replays. "Side by side" means within 200 m of another tracked boat on the same leg and the same tack or gybe, in the same breeze (the Side by side page of the fleet report has every stretch).

**Patterns across the regatta**
- **Upwind, side by side: fast and low.**
  - Against 1044 (32 min, 9 stretches): 1044 gained 131 m. Chomp was quicker (+33 m from speed), but 1044 made 164 m from height.
  - Against Mojo (13 min): Mojo gained 144 m. Chomp was a little quicker (+19 m), but Mojo made 163 m from height.
  - Race 1, beat 1 shows it clearly: on starboard Chomp did 5.97 kt at 49° to the course and 1044 5.58 kt at 41°, with Chomp at 14° of heel against 1044's 20°. On port it was 5.82 kt at 40° against 5.70 kt at 33°.
  - The exception proves the point: race 4 at 10:56, with 16° of heel, Chomp pointed 6° higher than 1044 and gained 21 m.
- **Downwind, side by side: the deepest of the three.**
  - Against 1044 (29 min): Chomp gained 25 m, +44 m from depth.
  - Against Mojo (39 min): Chomp gained 33 m, +62 m from depth, while Mojo was the faster boat.
- **Heel decides the beats.** Chomp's slow beats were at 12–15° (race 1 beat 2: 11.8°; race 3 beats 1 and 3: 14.6° and 13.5°); its good ones at 17–21°.
- **Tacks: frequent and expensive.** 8–17 a race, 32–36% speed loss and 16–27 s to recover. Race 1's eight tacks cost 160 m; race 5's seventeen cost 176 m.
- **Windward roundings: short layline calls.** Six layline tacks under 50 s from the mark, several overstood (6–23°). Race 4's second windward mark (205 m) was the costliest rounding of any boat.
- **Leeward roundings: good on Saturday, late gybes on Sunday.** Saturday's were 47–81 m. Sunday's first gates came after gybes 2–11 s from the mark, and cost 90–106 m.

**Race 1 (29th; 2nd of the three)**
- **Start:** middle (44% from the pin). The furthest back of the three: 109 m at −60 s and 19 m at the gun. Crossed 14 s late at 5.4 kt: the speed was there, the distance wasn't.
- **Beat 1 (14:34):**
  - The wind went left 9° over the beat and Chomp was mostly left (38% right). The right side to be on.
  - Heel 15.1°. Side by side with 1044 for 9½ minutes over two stretches, and 1044 gained 95 m, all on height.
- **Windward mark 1:** layline tack 20 s out, overstood 23°. 99 m lost.
- **Run 1:** one gybe. Side by side with Mojo from 18:07 for 5:37: Chomp gained 64 m, from speed and depth.
- **Leeward gate:** left-hand mark, out at 4.2 kt, 27 s to settle: 47 m, Chomp's best leeward rounding.
- **Beat 2 (15:25):**
  - Heel 11.8°, the flattest beat of anyone at PCC.
  - The wind went right 23°; Chomp was 44% right. From 36:44 it sailed 105 s on port headed about 10° without tacking.
  - Side by side with Mojo at 36:46: Mojo gained 26 m on height (Chomp at 62° to the course, heel 9.8°).
- **Run 2:** side by side with Mojo for 11 min; Chomp gained 26 m on speed.

**Race 2 (22nd, Chomp's best)**
- **Start:** middle, 0.7 m behind the line at the gun and accelerating (+1.3 kt through the gun). The best start of the three.
- **Beat 1 (15:17):**
  - Left (19% right) in a ±4° oscillation, with 6 tacks. Heel 18.7°.
  - Side by side with 1044 at 4:19 and 8:28 (7½ min): 1044 gained 16 m.
  - Ahead of 1044 at the first mark.
- **Windward mark 1:** in at 5.0 kt after a layline tack 37 s out. 103 m lost.
- **Run 1:** two gybes, and 29 s faster than 1044. Side by side with 1044 from 18:14 for 7 min: Chomp gained 42 m (6.50 against 6.35 kt, and deeper).
- **Leeward gate:** out at 4.9 kt, 51 s to settle.
- **Beat 2 (14:41):** heel 20.9°. Chomp went left (38% right) while 1044 went right (86%) and gained 50 s.
- **Windward mark 2:** overstood 9°. 102 m lost.
- **Run 2:** side by side with 1044 from 42:42 for 8 min; 1044 gained 42 m, from speed and depth both.

**Race 3 (29th)**
- **Start:** boat end (80% from the pin), 5.7 m back at the gun but at 1.8 kt. It was 2.3 kt at −10 s and 0.9 kt at +30 s: stalled on the line, which looks like being blanketed at the boat end. Crossed 10 s late.
- **Beat 1 (18:01): the right-hand corner.**
  - 96% right, up to 900 m out, at 14.6° of heel and a tacking angle of 86°. 2:24 behind 1044 at the first mark.
  - From 3:55 it sailed 45 s on port headed about 6° without tacking.
- **Run 1:** two gybes, 27 s faster than 1044.
- **Beat 2 (10:40):**
  - The fastest of the three. Left (13% right), heel 17.6°.
  - Side by side with Mojo at 27:23: Mojo gained 21 m on height (Chomp at 44° to the course against 32°), though Chomp was faster.
- **Windward mark 2:** 30 m lost, Chomp's best windward rounding.
- **Run 2:** Chomp gained 22 m on Mojo on the starboard gybe, then Mojo took 36 m back on port.
- **Leeward gate 2:** gybed 4 s out and came in at 2.8 kt, 24 m wide of the mark. 81 m lost.
- **Beat 3 (12:21):** heel 13.5°, 23% right. From 49:12 it sailed 51 s on starboard headed about 8°.

**Race 4 (31st)**
- **Start:** middle (59%), 12 m back at the gun, 6 s late at 5.4 kt. Slow at −30 s (3.6 kt, 64 m back).
- **Beat 1 (18:39):**
  - Heel 16.7°, 8 tacks.
  - Side by side with 1044 at 0:26 for 2:45: level (1044 +5 m).
  - At 10:56: Chomp gained 21 m on 1044, pointing 6° higher.
  - Side by side with Mojo at 11:42 and 17:22: Mojo gained 15 m.
  - 46 s behind 1044 at the first mark.
- **Run 1:** one gybe. Side by side with 1044 at 22:11: Chomp gained 26 m at 7.1 kt.
- **Leeward gate:** gybed 11 s out; out at 5.3 kt, but 84 s to settle. 106 m lost.
- **Beat 2 (15:58):**
  - Heel 20.6°. Hard left (4% right, up to 815 m out) while the wind went right 11°.
  - Side by side with Mojo at 43:05: Mojo gained 49 m in a minute, with Chomp 62° to the course.
- **Windward mark 2: the costliest rounding of the regatta.** Short of the layline, tacked 18 s before the mark at 4.7 kt and slowed to 3.5 kt. Out at 3.2 kt, 63 s to settle, 205 m lost.
- **Run 2:** a drag race with Mojo from 45:55 for 7:51, 19–49 m apart. Mojo gained 11 m. At the line Chomp was 2 s ahead by GPS, Mojo one place ahead officially.

**Race 5 (31st; 3rd of the three)**
- **Start:** middle (43%), 5.9 m back at the gun, 3 s late at 5.6 kt. It was at 1.7 kt at −30 s and built speed late.
- **Beat 1 (18:41): Chomp's best beat of the regatta.**
  - The wind went right 10°; Chomp went right (78%). Heel 19.7°, 4 tacks.
  - 1044 and Mojo were both over at the gun and restarted (+56 s and +52 s); Chomp was the only one of the three to start cleanly.
  - First of the three at the windward mark, 48 s ahead of 1044. From its restart, 1044 was 5 s quicker up the beat.
- **Run 1:** two gybes, 6.7 kt. 1044 gained 25 m side by side at 19:53, Chomp 18 m back at 26:50.
- **Leeward gate 1:** gybed 2 s out; out at 3.9 kt, 63 s to settle. 90 m lost.
- **Beat 2 (16:11):**
  - Left (9% right) in a ±5° oscillation, with the wind trending 5° right; Mojo went right (82%) and closed.
  - Heel 20.7° on starboard, 16.1° on port. From 42:29 it sailed 51 s on port headed about 10°.
  - Still first of the three at the second windward mark.
- **Windward mark 2:** overstood 12°, 77 s to settle. 91 m lost.
- **Run 2:**
  - Chomp gained 25 m on Mojo on the starboard gybe (deeper).
  - On port, Mojo gained 29 m (6.84 against 6.21 kt) and 17 m more from 52:11.
- **Beat 3 (16:15):**
  - 6 tacks and 48% right. This is where the lead went (+59 s on the fastest).
  - Side by side with 1044 at 58:58: 1044 gained 37 m on height, heeling 20.5° against Chomp's 16.5°.
  - Side by side with Mojo at 62:45: Mojo gained 20 m on height.
