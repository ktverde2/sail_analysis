## PCC 2026, Day 1 — fleet debrief (Feb 21, San Diego)
- Three tracked Etchells (1044, Chomp and Mojo), three windward-leewards with an offset at the top and a gate at the bottom, northwest breeze (about 285–316°).
- Results: 1044 1-1-1 (3 points), Chomp 2-2-2 (6), Mojo 3-3-3 (9).
- Over the day, Chomp was 5:23 behind 1044 and Mojo 8:32.
- The day was decided upwind. Against the race winners, Chomp lost 5:41 on the beats and Mojo 6:39. Starts were close to even, and downwind Chomp gained 18 s while Mojo lost 2:00.

**What went well**
- **1044: best upwind by a distance (5 of 7 beats won)**
  - The best angle over the ground (about 81° tacking angle, against 88–89°) at the same speed, on the shortest path (35% over the straight line, against 43%), and usually on the paying side.
- **Chomp: fastest downwind and out of the leeward marks**
  - Won the first run in races 2 and 3 by 27–29 s over 1044. Leeward exits at 4.2–4.9 kt, against 3.1–3.5 for 1044.
- **Mojo: the best starts and the cheapest tacks**
  - 7 s ahead of the race winners at the line over the day. 10–16 s to recover from a tack, against 17–27 s for Chomp.

**Top 3 to work on**
1. **1044: leeward exits and starts**
   - The slowest leeward exits in the fleet (3.1–3.5 kt; 73–108 s to get back to speed) and the most lost at leeward marks (89 m on average).
   - 10 s late from 115 m back in race 2, and over and restarted in race 3. Won anyway on upwind speed.
   - **Next time:** drop earlier, go wide in and tight out, and foot to full speed before pointing. Be within 2–4 lengths of the line at 2:00.
2. **Chomp: upwind angle and tacking**
   - The flattest boat upwind (15.6° average heel, against 18° for 1044) and about 8° wider over the ground. The two beats it won were at 17–18° of heel.
   - The most tacks (8–12 a race) and the slowest recovery (17–27 s).
   - One corner cost 2:24: race 3 beat 1, 100% right and 1 km out after a slow start.
   - **Next time:** sail with 17–18° of heel and more height (traveler up, jib a touch harder), tack only on clear headers, and get back to the middle after a bad start instead of banking a corner.
3. **Mojo: side choice and upwind mode**
   - Right of the rhumb line 78–91% of the time on four beats; on three of them the left or middle won (56, 44 and 47 s lost).
   - By compass it points highest in the fleet (67–70° tacking angle) but over the ground it tacks through 88°, the same as Chomp. About 9–10° of slip a tack, against 5.5–6° in July: a sign of pinching.
   - **Next time:** "speed before height": ease the jib off the inner mark and the inhaul off a notch until speed is back. Stay between the fleet and the mark unless the side is clear.

*The pattern of the day:*
- **The left paid on 4 of 7 beats, the right on 2, and the middle on 1**
  - Both beats in race 1 and race 2's first beat went left.
  - Race 2 beat 2 went right (1044 at 87% right beat Chomp at 36% by 50 s).
  - Race 3: middle on beat 1 (the right corner lost 2:24), left on beat 2 (the wind backed about 10°), right on the last beat.
- **Wind from the model feed ran low**
  - It showed 2.9 kt on race 1's first beat while boats sailed 5.6 kt upwind, so no one has speed targets for today. The fleet is the benchmark.
- **Offset marks at every windward mark**
  - Windward rounding losses (80–91 m on average) include the offset reach, which every boat sails. Compare boats with each other, not with July.

**Head to head**
- **1044 vs Chomp:** 2:28, 0:42 and 2:13.
  - Chomp led race 2 to the leeward gate; 1044 took it back on beat 2 by going right.
  - In races 1 and 3 the first beat decided it (+1:32 and +2:24 at the first mark).
- **Chomp vs Mojo:** Chomp by 49, 53 and 87 s.
  - Mojo was ahead at the first mark in races 1 and 3; Chomp passed on the second beat both times.
  - In race 2, Mojo's right-hand first beat put it 56 s behind at the first mark.

**Data notes**
- Speeds are over ground, from GPS.
- The logged wind on Mojo and 1044 is the same smoothed model feed (it changes only every few minutes). Chomp logs no wind. So there are no speed targets, and the comparisons are boat against boat.
- Mark passages and finishes come from the course marks for every boat. Njord's own fleet table has no legs for Chomp after race 1, and would show Mojo 2nd in races 2 and 3.
- Tacking angles over the ground come from GPS course, independent of each boat's compass. Heading-based slip figures depend on each heading sensor: Chomp's reads about 5° off, and 1044's reads unusually little slip.
- Shift calls come from each boat's own headings and are soft with few tacks. Which side paid is judged from how the boats on each side did.
