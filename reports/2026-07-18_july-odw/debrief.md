## July ODW, Saturday July 18, 2026 (San Diego)
Three windward-leewards in an 8–10 kt westerly around 263–268°, oscillating about ±4°. Starts close to the gun, speed on target, and one clear tactical pattern: we tacked off lifts and kept heading right.

**What went well**
- **Starts were close to the gun.** 4 s, 6 s and 1 s late, all mid-line. Race 1 accelerated well through the gun (+1.6 kt from −5 s to +5 s).
- **Speed on target.** 100–101% of the card at 9 kt in races 1 and 2 (96% in the lighter race 3), with heel within 2–3° of target. Much closer to the card than Sunday.
- **Laylines mostly good.** Four of five final tacks fetched the mark cleanly. The exception overstood by ~10° (race 2, first beat).

**Top 3 to work on**
1. **We tacked on lifts, not headers.** Of 13 tacks away from the start and marks, 6 were on a lift, only 2 on a header, and 5 had no clear shift. Five of the six left a lifted starboard tack to go onto port. In an oscillating breeze that's backwards: the lifted tack is the one to stay on. Race 3 is the clearest case: all three wind-call tacks were on lifts of 5–8°. **Next time:** the trimmer calls the compass number every 30 s on each tack. Tack when it's 5° worse than the beat's average, not before.
2. **The right side every time.** Every beat was sailed 70–100% right of the rhumb line. That's fine when the wind is drifting right, as it did by 4–5° in race 1. But on race 2's second beat the wind went 9° left and we were still 94% on the right. **Next time:** decide the side from the wind before the beat, and make "going right" a call you make each time, not a habit.
3. **Tack losses.** 31–37% speed loss per tack, worse than Sunday, and race 3's tacks took 21 s to recover (13 m lost each). **Next time:** slower helm in the lighter air; hold the jib until the bow is through, then trim hard.
